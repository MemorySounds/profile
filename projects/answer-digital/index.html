OVERVIEW

At Answer Digital (a consultancy of about 80 people), I worked as a consultant developer for three years, mainly in the health sector, starting as a Java developer and moving towards Node.js and full-stack JavaScript. I alternated between agile teams of 10-15 people and working on my own directly with clients, including several NHS trusts in Manchester and London and a Chinese bank. I was also part of the Answer Academy, mentoring junior developers through workshops and code reviews.

HIGHLIGHTED PROJECT

National data opt-out system. In England, patients can opt out of their data being used for research and planning, and hospital trusts have to remove them from datasets before sharing. The system sends NHS numbers to NHS Digital through its secure messaging service (MESH) and filters the trust's datasets with the result.

  • Took over a system a senior colleague had built for one trust and adapted it for a new London hospital trust: configuration, new backend features and JUnit tests.
  • Deployed it on the trust's own servers: Spring Boot services running as Windows services behind an IIS reverse proxy, connected to NHS Digital's MESH API.
  • Worked with patient lists of hundreds of thousands of records, and with the trust's staff and IT team on requirements and rollout.
  • Documented the setup and onboarded two junior developers before moving on.
OUTCOME

Delivered on time. The trust still uses it.
