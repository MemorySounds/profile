The Computer Firm (TCF)

Company Website
OVERVIEW

TCF is a small Swiss software firm. I worked with them from 2024 to 2025, mainly on academic publishing platforms and research tools in health.

PROJECT 1

Main developer on two academic journal platforms in musculoskeletal research, working closely with TCF's lead developer.

  • Editorial back-office: a submission and peer-review workflow with a history timeline and email notifications at each step, plus article, issue, author and user management.
  • Word (.docx) import that turns manuscripts into structured articles, including the reference list and links from in-text citations.
  • PDF generation for articles and whole issues (Puppeteer), and machine translation into six languages.
  • The public reading site, with search and social sharing.
THE HIVE AGA JOURNAL
PROJECT 2

A desktop note-taking app for surgeons (Electron and React) that runs entirely offline, for patient privacy. I built the local speech features: a small Python (FastAPI) text-to-speech service using Piper, packaged into the app, and local Whisper for dictation.
Further details under NDA.
