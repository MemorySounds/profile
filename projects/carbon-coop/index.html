Carbon Co-op

Company Website
OVERVIEW

Carbon Co-op is a UK co-operative helping households cut their carbon emissions. In a short engagement, I built a Python integration for the open-source Home Assistant platform that polls their energy-data API at intervals, so households can monitor their energy use. It was part of a larger project with an EU consortium of energy initiatives.

MY ROLE
  • Designed and built the integration, which ran on a small home energy box that Carbon Co-op was producing.
