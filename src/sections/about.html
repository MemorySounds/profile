About me

After graduating in 2019 from University of Leeds with First Class Honours in Computer Science, I spent three years at Answer Digital, starting as a Java developer before gradually moving toward Node.js and full-stack JavaScript. I worked on a variety of projects in the health sector, from web-apps to data retrieval and reporting tools.

These days I work across the full stack, mostly on the backend: data models, APIs, background jobs and integrations with other systems, with React on the front end. I write JavaScript and TypeScript with Node.js day to day, and I’ve shipped Java with Spring Boot. I like keeping things practical and reliable, and I’m drawn to work with a social or environmental purpose, but above all I want to build software that genuinely helps the people using it.

Since moving to Paris, I’ve worked as a freelance software developer with start-ups, co-ops and charities, on projects ranging from academic publishing platforms and a charity webshop to dataspace connectors for EU research. I’m now looking to join a product team for the long term. Alongside my freelance work, I’ve been a network member of Code-Operative (a UK-based tech co-op) and HappyDev Paris (a Paris cell of the French network of freelance software developers), both have now sadly wrapped up their endeavors. I am now an active member of SEPHEO — an evolving co-op for freelancers.
